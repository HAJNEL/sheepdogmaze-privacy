Sheepdog Maze – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Sheepdog Maze (“the game”) is made by Classic Games Studios (“we”). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is for kids and adults.

In short
  • There are no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or contact details.
  • Your game progress and your birth year are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. For players under 13 (under 16 in Europe), ads are child-directed and non-personalized, and the game doesn't ask to track.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

WhatWhy
Game progress: stars, scores, coins, abilities, cosmetics and settingsSo you can keep playing where you left off.
The birth year you enter on first launchTo decide whether ads must be child-directed. It's asked once and never leaves your device.
Ad timing (when the last ad was shown)So ads aren't shown too often.

We don't have servers for this game, so none of this is sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads between some levels and optional reward videos. Google may collect information from your device to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This can include an ad identifier, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Children: if the birth year shows a player is under 13 (under 16 in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), every ad request is marked as child-directed. Google then shows only non-personalized, family-safe ads and limits the data it collects. The game doesn't show Apple's tracking prompt or a consent form to these players.
  • Older players: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and some US states), Google's consent form asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choices later in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. The game may also show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the ad identifier isn't shared, and you can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • All ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated “G”).
  • Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between levels. Reward videos stay available, but only if you choose to watch them.

Purchases

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Parents can require approval for purchases with Ask to Buy.

Children's privacy

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, chat or ways to share personal information. Ads for children are handled as described above. If you're a parent and believe information about your child has been collected, contact us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to the use of personal information. Because we don't hold any information about you, most requests are best directed to Google (for ads) or Apple (for purchases). You can delete everything the game stores by deleting the game, or erase game progress in Settings → Reset progress.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
