Playing

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your sheepdog. Sheep run away from the dog, so get behind them and chase them through the gate of a pen. Pen enough of the flock before the clock runs out to pass the field. There's also a How to guide on the main menu.

What are stars and coins?

Stars come from beating your best score on a field. They unlock new abilities in the Kennel and open new farms. Coins are found on the fields, and also come from 3-star results and a daily gift. They pay for ability training, extras like the Red Bandana, and handy boosts.

I'm stuck on a field.

Try a Hint (gold trails to the pens) or an Extra ability from the field list. After a few tries you can also skip the field and come back for its stars later.

Ads and purchases

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Remove ads in the game. It's a one-time purchase that removes the ads between fields and includes 200 coins. Meadow Farm never has ads. Optional reward videos stay available, but they only play if you choose to watch them.

I bought Remove Ads but I'm still seeing ads.

Open Settings → Restore purchases while you're connected to the internet, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. If the purchase is waiting for a parent's approval (Ask to Buy), ads will go away once it's approved.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law gives you a choice, the game shows Settings → Ad privacy choices. To change whether apps can track you, go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

For parents

On first launch the game asks for the player's birth year, and the answer stays on the device. For players under 13 (under 16 in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), ads are child-directed and non-personalized, and the game doesn't ask to track. All ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. There are no accounts, chat or ways to share personal information. You can require approval for purchases with Ask to Buy.

The birth-year answer is asked only once. To answer it again, delete and reinstall the game. This also erases game progress.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone, not online. Deleting the game deletes your progress, but Remove Ads can always be restored for free.
